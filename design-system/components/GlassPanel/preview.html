Real lensing — SVG displacement, showcase only

bent, not just blurred

The striped background doesn't just blur under this panel — it visibly warps, the way a real lens bends a straight line passing behind it. That's backdrop-filter: url(#glass-lens) blur() saturate(), an SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap chain. It's the closest a browser gets to Apple's real-time refraction, renders reliably in Chromium, and is expensive — reserve it for one hero moment (an onboarding card, a splash), never the whole dashboard. Every other glass surface in Strydr uses the cheaper default recipe above: a corner glint plus a two-tone bevel baked into shadow-glass, which gets 90% of the read at a fraction of the cost.

Motion — press and touch illumination

Liquid Glass is gel-like, not rigid — a tap scales the surface down to .965 on a bouncy --ease-glass curve, lights up where it was touched, and settles back out on release. See the Motion card for the full interaction set: morphing between shapes and a scroll-responsive bar.
