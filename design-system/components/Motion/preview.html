Morph — a control grows into what it needs

Click the icon. A circular control and a search field are the same shape family (capsule — Apple's default Liquid Glass shape) at two widths, not two components, so growing from one into the other is a single width transition on --ease-glass rather than a cut. Real Liquid Glass does this with a shared glassEffectID so the system animates between two distinct views; this demo fakes the same result by resizing one element, which is the right call unless you're actually in SwiftUI.
